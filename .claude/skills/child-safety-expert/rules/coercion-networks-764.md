# "764" and "Com"-style coercion networks

Loose online networks (often named "764" or grouped under "The Com") that target vulnerable minors, frequently through Discord, Roblox, Telegram and gaming communities. Members compete for status by coercing victims into self-harm, sexual content, animal cruelty or violence, then use that content for extortion. US authorities have warned about them repeatedly since 2023 and have charged members with child exploitation and terrorism-related offences (see `real-world-cases.md` for dated, sourced examples).

## How it shows up in a child's DMs
- Approach in vent, self-harm, "depression", gore, or edgy servers; or after the child posts about sadness.
- Fake friendship and "understanding" → requests for photos → threats ("doxx", "I'll send this to your family").
- Demands to cut, carve names or "signs" into skin, write in blood, film it ("cut sign", "fansign", "lore"), or harm pets/siblings.
- Owner/pet/slave language combined with harm ("you belong to me", "obey").
- Pressure to go live on camera, or to join a call where others watch.
- Glorification of suicide or of previous victims.

## Distinguish from trading slang
In Roblox trading servers, "owner", "proof", "staff", "leak" and "blacklist" are ordinary words. The 764 pattern is **harm + control + recording**, not those words alone.

## Response
Critical. Save evidence (screenshots of messages and usernames), do not let the child keep engaging, report to the platform and national police/cyber unit, and get urgent mental-health support for the child — these victims are often already self-harming.
