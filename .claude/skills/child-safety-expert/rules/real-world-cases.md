# Real-World Cases: Child Harm via Discord and Roblox (2021–2026)

**Purpose.** This file grounds the child-safety skill in real, publicly documented harm patterns on Discord and Roblox. When the assistant reviews a child's Discord DMs with a parent's consent, it should be able to spot tactics that have actually come before grooming, sextortion, coerced self-harm and account theft, and it should not invent threats that aren't in the messages. Entries draw on government releases (US DOJ/FBI, NCMEC, state attorneys general, UK NCA/CPS/courts, Australia's eSafety, India's CBI), filed lawsuits, and major investigative journalism. Child victims are never named, and descriptions are kept non-graphic on purpose. **Allegations in lawsuits, complaints and indictments are unproven unless a plea or conviction is noted.** Compiled November 2026. Case status may have changed since then.

**Confidence tags:** `[High]` means a primary or official source was checked directly. `[Med]` means reputable secondary reporting, or a primary source seen only through search summaries. `[Low]` means single-source, conflicting, or non-authoritative: treat it as indicative only.

## 1. Grooming by adults

### 1.1 NBC News investigation: Discord in abductions, grooming and sextortion
- **Date / place / platforms:** Jun 2023 · US-wide · Discord (with Roblox and Minecraft as entry points)
- **What happened:** NBC News found 35 cases over six years in which adults were prosecuted for kidnapping, grooming or sexual assault that allegedly involved Discord communications. Twenty-two of them came during or after the pandemic, and at least 15 ended in guilty pleas or verdicts. It found another 165 prosecutions for CSAM or sextortion involving Discord, at least 91 of which ended in pleas or verdicts. Several cases started in a video game and moved to Discord, and some children were taken across state lines after months of grooming.
- **Visible chat signals:** an invitation to move from a game to Discord; small private servers with little moderation; an adult posing as a minor; months of rapport before any request; image requests followed by threats.
- **Outcome:** Discord said it does "not monitor every server or every conversation." It also said it could not yet automatically detect newly created CSAM or grooming messages, and that it was working with Thorn on grooming detection.
- **Source:** Ben Goggin, NBC News investigation, 21 Jun 2023 — https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/social-media/discord-child-safety-social-platform-challenges-rcna89769 `[High]`

### 1.2 Bloomberg Businessweek: "Roblox's Pedophile Problem"
- **Date / place / platforms:** Jul 2024 · US · Roblox (contact often moved to other apps)
- **What happened:** Reporters Olivia Carville and Cecilia D'Anastasio counted at least 24 US arrests between 2018 and 2024 of people who abducted or sexually abused children they had groomed through Roblox. Current and former staff described policing predators as a "Sisyphean task."
- **Visible chat signals:** offers of Robux in exchange for photos or real-world meetings.
- **Outcome:** Roblox said the report contained "glaring mischaracterizations." The piece was followed by the Hindenburg short report and state lawsuits (see §6).
- **Source:** Bloomberg Businessweek — https://www.bloomberg.com/features/2024-roblox-pedophile-problem/ (paywalled; findings confirmed via Game Developer and others) `[Med]`

### 1.3 Indiana teen transported to New Jersey after grooming on games and social apps
- **Date / place / platforms:** First contact Feb 2022, arrest May 2022, sentenced Aug. 25 (2023 per the case timeline; verify) · S.D. Indiana / New Jersey · Instagram, Roblox, Discord (per the DOJ summary)

- **Visible chat signals:** a fake persona; gifts and money; pressure to keep the relationship secret; logistics talk (address, "I'll get you a ride").
- **Outcome:** He pleaded guilty to transporting a minor and to coercion/enticement. He was sentenced to 180 months, 20 years of supervised release, and $10,000 in restitution.
- **Source:** USAO S.D. Ind., "New Jersey Man Sentenced to 15 Years in Federal Prison After Grooming Minor Online…" — https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdin/pr/new-jersey-man-sentenced-15-years-federal-prison-after-grooming-minor-online-and ; WTHR coverage `[Med]`. Claims that he was a Roblox "developer" come from YouTubers and are unverified `[Low]`.

### 1.4 10-year-old taken 250+ miles by a man met on Roblox and Discord
- **Date / place / platforms:** Apr 2025 · Kern County → Elk Grove, California · Roblox, Discord
- **What happened:** A 10-year-old went missing overnight. Detectives found her messages with a 27-year-old man who had talked with her on Roblox and Discord, and police found her in his car more than 250 miles away the same day.
- **Visible chat signals:** a much older "friend" from a game who moves the chat to Discord, plans a meetup, and asks the child to slip out at night.
- **Outcome:** Matthew Macatuno Naval was charged with kidnapping and lewd acts. He was held to answer on three counts and pleaded not guilty. The case was pending as of the last report found. The Sheriff urged parents to know who their children talk to in games.
- **Source:** NBC News, "California man accused of kidnapping 10-year-old he met on Roblox" (17 Apr 2025) — https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/kidnapping-roblox-rcna201795 ; BakersfieldNow on the preliminary hearing `[High]`

## 2. Financial sextortion and image-based extortion

### 2.1 FBI/HSI/NCMEC national public safety alert
- **Date / place:** Dec 2022 · US
- **What happened:** Over the previous year, law enforcement received more than 7,000 reports of financial sextortion of minors. These involved at least 3,000 victims, mostly boys, and more than a dozen suicides. Many schemes came from West Africa (Nigeria, Côte d'Ivoire). Offenders get a minor to send an image, then demand money or gift cards, and often release the images even after being paid.
- **Visible chat signals:** a flirtatious stranger, often posing as a girl; quick escalation; "I'll show you first"; then a threat and a payment demand with a deadline.
- **Source:** FBI press release, "FBI and Partners Issue National Public Safety Alert on Financial Sextortion Schemes" — https://www.fbi.gov/news/press-releases/fbi-and-partners-issue-national-public-safety-alert-on-financial-sextortion-schemes `[High]`

### 2.2 FBI: "The Financially Motivated Sextortion Threat" and FBI guidance
- **Date / place:** Jan 2024, plus the standing FBI guidance page · US
- **What happened:** From Oct 2021 to Mar 2023 the FBI and HSI received more than 13,000 reports involving at least 12,600 victims, mostly boys. The FBI says it knows of more than 20 suicides; the reprint gives at least 14 for that window, and the figures differ by publication. FBI guidance says these schemes often **start on gaming sites and apps**, where an offer of game credits or codes can be enough to get a first image, and it flags a request to **move to another platform** as a warning sign. Demands are typically for gift cards, mobile or peer-to-peer payments, wire transfers or crypto. Paying does not stop the extortion.
- **Visible chat signals:** "I'll give you a gift card or credits for a pic"; "let's move to [other app]"; a countdown; "pay or I post it."
- **Outcome / advice:** Don't pay. Don't delete the account or messages (keep them as evidence). Report and block; contact the FBI (tips.fbi.gov, 1-800-CALL-FBI) or NCMEC (report.cybertip.org); use NCMEC's Take It Down to remove images.
- **Source:** FBI, "Financially Motivated Sextortion" — https://www.fbi.gov/how-we-can-help-you/common-frauds-and-scams/sextortion/financially-motivated-sextortion ; FBI Charlotte release, 18 Jan 2024 — https://www.fbi.gov/contact-us/field-offices/charlotte/news/financially-motivated-sextortion-cases-on-the-rise-fbi-charlotte-warns `[Med]` (the FBI pages blocked direct fetch, so content was confirmed via search and reprints)

### 2.3 Thorn and NCMEC research: "Trends in Financial Sextortion"
- **Date / place:** Jun 2024 · US (NCMEC CyberTipline data)
- **What happened:** NCMEC was receiving about 812 sextortion reports a week, and more than two-thirds involved financial demands. Where age and gender were known, 90% of financial sextortion victims were boys aged 14–17. Instagram and Snapchat were the most common platforms. Tactics included catfishing, sending imagery first to prompt reciprocation, threats to hack accounts, and AI-generated fakes. Escalation often happened within hours of first contact. More than 1 in 6 reports that described impacts mentioned self-harm or suicide.
- **Source:** Thorn, "New research from Thorn: Financial sextortion on the rise, targeting teen boys" (24 Jun 2024) — https://www.thorn.org/blog/new-research-from-thorn-financial-sextortion-on-the-rise-targeting-teen-boys/ ; NBC News — https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/security/financial-sextortion-often-targets-teen-boys-instagram-according-new-d-rcna157790 `[High]`

### 2.4 Discord-based image extortion (US and India)
- **SDNY, conduct Aug 2024, arrest 29 Apr 2025:** A 23-year-old from Newburgh NY allegedly contacted a minor on Discord, sent her nude photos of herself that she had taken earlier, and threatened to share them with friends and family unless she sent more. He was charged with extortionate interstate communications. A later 6-year sentence was reported by one outlet only `[Low]`. Source: https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/us-attorney-charges-newburgh-man-using-discord-platform-extort-sexually-explicit `[High]`
- **India, CBI "Operation Hawk-2025," May 2025:** Acting on intelligence from US authorities, the CBI arrested two men, in Mangalore and Delhi. Each allegedly used Discord aliases (one used several Discord IDs) to coerce a US minor into sending explicit material, then threatened to leak it. They were charged under the IPC, IT Act and POCSO. Source: The Tribune (India), "CBI nabs 2 who sexually exploited US girls via messaging app" (1 May 2025) — https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/cbi-nabs-2-who-sexually-exploited-us-girls-via-messaging-app `[Med]`
- **Visible chat signals:** a stranger who already holds images of the child (stolen or leaked); "send more or I send these to your friends and family"; multiple alt accounts reappearing after a block.

### 2.5 Wrongful-death suit: a 15-year-old groomed on Roblox, threatened on Discord
- **Date / place / platforms:** Filed 12 Sep 2025 · San Francisco County Superior Court · Roblox → Discord
- **What happened (alleged):** An adult posing as a child befriended a 12-year-old on Roblox. He urged the boy to turn off parental controls and move to Discord, where he repeatedly demanded explicit images and threatened to share them. The boy died by suicide in 2024. Police later told the family the man had been arrested in Florida for exploiting other children through the same apps.
- **Visible chat signals:** "turn off your parental controls"; "add me on Discord"; repeated image demands; threats to post.
- **Outcome:** Pending. Claims include wrongful death and fraudulent concealment. Roblox and Discord pointed to their safety measures and declined to comment on the litigation.
- **Source:** NBC News, "Roblox, Discord sued after 15-year-old boy was allegedly groomed online before he died by suicide" — https://www.nbcnews.com/news/us-news/roblox-discord-sued-15-year-old-boy-was-allegedly-groomed-online-died-rcna231049 `[High]`

### 2.6 Australia eSafety: platforms' sextortion detection gaps
- **Date / place:** 2026 transparency reporting · Australia
- **What happened:** eSafety found that several services, Discord among them, were not using language analysis to detect sexual extortion; Discord had trialled it and stopped. eSafety received more than 2,000 sextortion complaints between Jul and Dec 2025, most from young men aged 18–24, and the Commissioner said scammers were targeting boys as young as 15.
- **Source:** Cyber Daily — https://www.cyberdaily.au/culture/13895-esafety-report-finds-big-tech-not-adequately-protecting-young-men-from-sextortion ; AdNews; Women's Agenda `[Med]` (the eSafety primary report was not read directly)

## 3. "764" and "Com" networks

### 3.1 FBI PSA: violent online groups extort minors into self-harm and CSAM
- **Date:** 12 Sep 2023 (I-091223-PSA) · US
- **What happened:** The FBI warned that groups target minors aged 8–17, especially LGBTQ+ youth, racial minorities and children with depression or suicidal thoughts. They extort victims into producing CSAM, self-harm footage, animal cruelty or violence, using threats to "swat," dox, or share images with family. Some members aim to push victims into live-streamed suicide.
- **Source:** IC3, "Violent Online Groups Extort Minors to Self-Harm and Produce Child Sexual Abuse Material" — https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2023/PSA230912 `[High]`

### 3.2 FBI PSAs: "764" networks and "The Com" factions
- **Date:** 6 Mar 2025 (I-030625-PSA) and 23 Jul 2025 · US
- **What happened:** In March 2025 the FBI warned of a sharp rise in 764-type networks. Typical victims are 10–17 (some as young as 9), often girls, and especially children with depression, eating disorders or suicidal thoughts. Offenders first build trust or a romance, then escalate to shaming, isolation and extortion. The PSA lists warning signs including **a new online contact the child is infatuated with or afraid of, anonymous gifts (money, gaming currency, virtual items)**, words or symbols cut into the skin, harmed pets, and police arriving under false pretenses (swatting). The July 2025 PSA splits "The Com" into Hacker Com, IRL Com and Extortion Com, and says many members are minors.
- **Source:** IC3, "Violent Online Networks Target Vulnerable and Underage Populations Across the United States and Around the Globe" — https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2025/PSA250306 `[High]` ; IC3 PSA on The Com — https://www.ic3.gov/PSA/2025/PSA250723-3 `[Med]`

### 3.3 764 founder sentenced (Texas)
- **Date / place / platforms:** 2023 · Erath County, Texas · Discord
- **What happened:** Bradley Cadenhead started the 764 Discord server as a teenager, reportedly at about 15. It grew into a network that coerced children into self-harm and sexual content. Discord repeatedly banned his accounts.
- **Outcome:** He pleaded guilty to possession of child pornography and was sentenced to 80 years in state prison. A later habeas challenge was denied.
- **Source:** WaPo/Wired/Der Spiegel/Recorder joint investigation (see 4.1); Wikipedia summary of court records `[Med]`

### 3.4 Michigan 764 member ran "Sewer" Discord servers
- **Date / place / platforms:** Sentenced 7 Nov 2024 · W.D. Michigan · Discord
- **What happened:** Richard Densmore, 47, known as "Rabid," ran Discord servers and chat rooms that recruited children and pressured them into self-harm and sexual acts. DOJ called 764 a violent extremist network.
- **Outcome:** He pleaded guilty to sexual exploitation of a child and received 30 years (the statutory maximum).
- **Source:** DOJ OPA, "Member of Violent 764 Terror Network Sentenced to 30 Years in Prison for Sexually Exploiting a Child" — https://www.justice.gov/archives/opa/pr/member-violent-764-terror-network-sentenced-30-years-prison-sexually-exploiting-child ; Michigan Public `[High]`

- **What happened:** Prasan Nepal, 20, and Leonidas Varagiannis, 21, were charged with running a child exploitation enterprise through "764 Inferno," a core subgroup that worked over encrypted apps. Coverage reported more than 250 764-related FBI investigations across 55 field offices.
- **Outcome:** Charged, and presumed innocent. Reports conflict on whether Varagiannis will be extradited from Greece `[Low]`.
- **Source:** DOJ OPA, "Leaders of 764 Arrested and Charged for Operating Global Child Exploitation Enterprise" (30 Apr 2025) — https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/leaders-764-arrested-and-charged-operating-global-child-exploitation-enterprise `[High]`

### 3.6 "Greggy's Cult," a Discord precursor network to 764
- **Date:** Indictment unsealed 2 Dec 2025 · E.D.N.Y.
- **What happened:** Five alleged leaders were charged with child exploitation offenses for running Discord servers whose members coerced victims into degrading acts and self-harm and repeatedly urged them to kill themselves. DOJ says the group predated 764 and that some 764 leaders were former members.

- **Date / place / platforms:** Verdict 9 Oct 2026 · Paris · Discord
- **What happened:** Rohan Rane, 29, an Indian national, was convicted as the leader of "CVLT," a Discord group that targeted girls in the US, UK and Canada. About 20 victims were identified. He was arrested in 2021 after a tip from US authorities. The FBI lists CVLT among 764-type networks.
- **Outcome:** 20 years, reported as the maximum available.
- **Source:** AP via ABC30 — https://abc30.com/post/rohan-rane-sentenced-paris-france-global-discord-ring-known-cvlt-accused-child-abuse/19927917/ ; CTV News `[Med]`

### 3.10 UK National Crime Agency warning on "Com" networks
- **Date:** 25 Mar 2025 (National Strategic Assessment) · UK
- **What happened:** UK reports of Com networks rose sixfold from 2022 to 2024. Offenders are mostly teenage boys who share sadistic and misogynistic material and target people their own age or younger. In some cases girls as young as 11 were coerced into harming themselves, their siblings or their pets. The NCA urged parents to know what their children do online.
- **Source:** NCA, "Sadistic online harm groups putting people at 'unprecedented risk', warns the NCA" — https://nationalcrimeagency.gov.uk/news/sadistic-online-harm-groups-putting-people-at-unprecedented-risk-warns-the-nca `[Med]`

## 4. Self-harm and suicide coercion

### 4.1 Washington Post–led investigation into 764
- **Date / platforms:** Mar 2024 · Discord, Telegram (with some Roblox and Minecraft presence)
- **What happened:** A joint investigation by The Post, Wired, Der Spiegel and Recorder found an international network that seeks out children with mental-health struggles and blackmails them into hurting themselves on camera. Some victims were made to cut or write offenders' usernames on their bodies. Discord said it removed 34,000 accounts associated with the group the previous year.
- **Visible chat signals:** being "chosen" by a group; requests to prove loyalty; demands for self-harm photos or video; offenders' usernames used as "signatures."
- **Source:** Washington Post, "On popular online platforms, predatory groups coerce children into self-harm" — https://www.washingtonpost.com/investigations/interactive/2024/764-predator-discord-telegram/ `[High]`

### 4.2 US 13-year-old's livestreamed death → German murder trial and suit against Discord
- **Date / place / platforms:** Death 2022 (Washington State); Hamburg arrest Jun 2025; charges Oct 2025; trial from Jan 2026 · Discord (NCMEC's 2021 referral contained Discord chat transcripts)
- **What happened:** German prosecutors charged a 21-year-old ("White Tiger," Shahriar J.) with 204 offences. They include murder as an "indirect perpetrator" over the US boy's livestreamed suicide and five attempted murders. Prosecutors say he found vulnerable children in chats and games, built bonds with them, then exploited them; more than 30 victims were alleged. The family has separately sued Discord in Washington state court, alleging it had prior warnings about 764.
- **Visible chat signals:** an online "friend" who becomes controlling; encouragement to self-harm; group pressure to "go live."
- **Outcome:** The trial runs behind closed doors and is scheduled through Dec 2026. The defence calls the murder theory unprovable. Discord said disrupting 764 is "among Discord's highest priorities."
- **Source:** CBS News, "Man dubbed 'White Tiger' charged with murder over U.S. teen's livestreamed suicide" — https://www.cbsnews.com/news/man-charged-murder-germany-us-teen-suicide-764-network ; ABC News, "Parents of 764 victim file wrongful death lawsuit against Discord" — https://abc7ny.com/post/parents-764-victim-file-wrongful-death-lawsuit-discord/18626544/ `[High]`/`[Med]`

### 4.3 England: 764 member urged a girl to livestream her suicide
- **Date / place / platforms:** Sentenced 16 Jan 2025 · Old Bailey · Telegram (chat with the victim); Telegram, Discord and Snapchat (with other 764 members)
- **What happened:** A 19-year-old from Horsham encouraged a girl who said she was considering suicide to do it on video "for me," so he could claim it for 764.
- **Outcome:** Cameron Finnigan pleaded guilty to encouraging or assisting suicide, a terrorism-document offence, indecent images and criminal damage. He received 6 years in custody plus a 3-year extended licence.
- **Source:** R v Finnigan sentencing remarks — https://www.judiciary.uk/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/R-v-Finnigan.pdf ; PA via Express & Star `[Med]`

### 4.4 England: first child-victim conviction for encouraging serious self-harm (OSA s.184)
- **Date / place / platforms:** Sentenced 20 Oct 2025 · Manchester Crown Court · Discord (first contact, per The Guardian) and six Snapchat accounts (per the CPS)
- **What happened:** A 42/43-year-old groomed an "extremely vulnerable" girl, about 13 when it began, with compliments and trust-building. When she refused to send explicit material he switched to threats, asked her for self-harm videos, and later supplied her with a blade.

## 5. Scams targeting kids

### 5.1 "Free Nitro" QR codes and QR-login account takeover (Discord)
- **Pattern:** A DM offers free Nitro and a QR code. Scanning it with the Discord app approves a login session for the scammer, which can bypass the password and 2FA. The hijacked account is then used to message friends, which makes the next scam look trusted.
- **Visible chat signals:** "scan this to claim your Nitro gift"; urgency; the message comes from a friend acting out of character.
- **Source:** Discord blog, "Protecting Against Scams on Discord" (3 Feb 2022): don't scan QR codes from people you can't verify; never share your password or token — https://discord.com/blog/common-scams-what-to-look-out-for ; Kaspersky — https://www.kaspersky.com/resource-center/threats/discord-scams `[Med]`

### 5.2 "I accidentally reported you" and fake Discord staff
- **Pattern:** A friend, often a hacked account, says they "accidentally reported" you and that you'll be banned unless you contact a "Discord employee." The fake staffer sends a fake support screenshot and asks for a verification code, an account change, or payment, then takes over the account.
- **Key facts:** Discord's safety guidance says staff **never** DM users for support, and an official message **never** asks for a password or token and carries an "OFFICIAL"/system badge.
- **Source:** Discord Safety Center, "Understanding and Avoiding Common Scams" — https://discord.com/safety/understanding-and-avoiding-common-scams ; Discord Help, "Discord Official Messages" — https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036118732-Discord-Official-Messages ; Bitdefender (27 Feb 2026) — https://www.bitdefender.com/en-us/blog/hotforsecurity/discord-scam-accidentally-reported `[High]`

### 5.3 "Try my game" / fake downloads / token grabbers
- **Pattern:** A friend or server member asks the child to test a game, mod or "cheat." The file is info-stealing malware that hijacks Discord and other accounts and then messages everyone on the friends list. Discord's own guidance: don't run programs or paste code you don't recognize.
- **Source:** Discord blog (above); Moonlock — https://moonlock.com/discord-scams `[Low]` (security-vendor and community sources; widely reported but no government case found)

### 5.4 Roblox "beaming": stealing valuable items from children
- **Date:** Feb 2022 · Motherboard/VICE
- **Pattern:** "Beamers" pick accounts that hold valuable limited items, send phishing links to fake Roblox login pages or lookalike domains, strip the items, and resell them for real money or crypto. Contact often happens in game servers or Discord communities.
- **Source:** VICE/Motherboard (Joseph Cox), "How Roblox 'Beamers' Get Rich Stealing from Children" — https://www.vice.com/en/article/88gd4a/roblox-beaming-hackers ; Malwarebytes — https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/privacy-2/2022/02/roblox-beamers-steal-items-from-kids/ `[Med]`

### 5.5 Copy-paste "free items" code and double-trade scams (Cisco Talos)
- **Date:** Dec 2023 · US
- **Pattern:** (a) A scammer gives the child code or text to paste for "free items," which actually hands over account access. (b) The scammer sets up two trades, one that looks good for the child (and is rigged to fail) and one that favours the scammer (which goes through). Stolen items are resold in resale chat rooms.
- **Source:** CBS Texas, "Players beware: Tech research firm warns of Roblox scams" (29 Dec 2023) — https://www.cbsnews.com/texas/news/players-beware-tech-research-firm-warns-of-roblox-scams `[Med]`

### 5.6 Ukraine: 610,000+ Roblox accounts stolen with fake "cheats"
- **Date:** Charges announced Sep 2026 · Lviv region, Ukraine
- **What happened:** Three suspects, the organiser 19 years old, allegedly spread info-stealing malware disguised as game advantages or free bonuses. They stole session cookies to take over more than 610,000 Roblox accounts (May 2025–Apr 2026) and sold them to buyers in Russia. Estimated proceeds were about $480,000. They face up to 12 years.
- **Source:** The Record, Recorded Future News — https://therecord.media/ukraine-roblox-hacker-arrested `[High]`

### 5.7 Free Robux generators, off-platform trades, "middlemen" and cross-trading
- **Roblox's position:** Any offer of free Robux or items is a scam and violates the Terms of Use. Deals made outside the official Trade/Sell features get **no recovery help**.
- **Pattern (lower-confidence sources):** High-value limited trades get arranged on Discord with a "trusted middleman" who is really the scammer's alt or friend. Cross-trading (Roblox items for items in another game, or for real money) is generally against the rules, which leaves victims with no recourse.
- **Source:** Roblox Help, "Free Robux or Subscription Generators" — https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/204262550-Free-Robux-or-Subscription-Generators ; Roblox, "Avoiding Frauds & Scams" — https://about.roblox.com/frauds-and-scams `[High]`; middleman pattern via parent guides and game-support pages such as Adopt Me support `[Low]`

## 7. Patterns to look for in a child's DMs

Example phrasings below are **paraphrased and generic**. Use them to recognize intent, not as exact-match keywords. Context matters: two kids joking is not an adult grooming a child. Weigh **who** is speaking (a known peer, or a stranger met in a game), **how fast** things escalate, and whether **several** signals appear together.

### 7.1 Grooming and contact escalation

| Tactic | Example phrasing (paraphrased) | Why it matters | Seen in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform migration | "Roblox chat is too strict, add me on Discord" / "let's talk on Snap/WhatsApp instead" | Moves the child to less-moderated, parent-invisible DMs. The most consistent step across cases. | 1.1, 1.4, 1.5, 1.7, 2.5, 3.8, AR suit |
| Turning off safeguards | "turn off your parental controls" / "make a new account so your mom can't see" | Deliberately removes oversight. | 2.5 |
| Age or identity deception | "I'm 15 too" / "I'm a girl from near you"; voice that "sounds young" | Lowers the child's guard. Adults have posed as peers and used voice changers. | 1.3, 1.5, 1.6 |
| Flattery / special bond | "you're so mature for your age" / "you're the only one who gets me" | Builds dependence and isolates the child from peers and parents. | FBI 3.2, CPS 4.4 |
| Gifts that create debt | "I'll send you Robux/Nitro/a gift card" / "want me to buy you that limited?" | Gifts are later "owed" back, often as photos. The FBI lists anonymous gaming-currency gifts as a warning sign. | 1.2, 1.5, 1.6, 3.2 |
| Secrecy | "don't tell your parents, they won't understand" / "delete our chats" | Prevents discovery. Often precedes sexual requests. | 1.3, 1.7 |
| Boundary testing | "are you alone?" / "what are you wearing?" / body questions / "truth or dare" | Normalises sexual topics before image requests. | 1.1, 1.7 |
| Location and routine | "what school / town / street?" / "send a pic out your window" / "when do your parents sleep?" | Precedes in-person meetings or abduction. | 1.3, 1.4, 1.6 |
| Meetup logistics | "I'll get you an Uber" / "I'm driving to your area" / "sneak out tonight" | **Imminent physical danger. Alert the parent immediately.** | 1.3, 1.4, 1.6 |
| Pressure after disclosure | "tell them nothing happened" / "drop it or else" | Witness intimidation. | 1.7, 3.8 |

### 7.2 Sextortion and image-based extortion

| Tactic | Example phrasing (paraphrased) | Why it matters | Seen in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rapid escalation from a stranger | flirting within minutes → "send a pic," often "I'll go first" | Financial sextortion often escalates within hours of first contact. | 2.1, 2.3 |
| Reciprocity bait | "I showed you mine, now you" | Creates false obligation. | NCMEC, 2.3 |
| Threat with leverage | "I have your friends list / followers" / "I'll send this to your school and family" | The core extortion step. The child may be terrified and hide it. | 2.1, 2.4, 2.5 |
| Payment demand + deadline | "send a gift card code / Robux / Cash App in 10 minutes or it's posted" | Financial sextortion. **Paying doesn't stop it.** | 2.1, 2.2 |
| Demand for "more" | "send more or I leak these" | Sexual extortion, often non-financial. | 2.4, 2.5, 3.8 |
| Fake or AI imagery threats | "I'll make fake nudes of you" | Works even when the child never sent anything. | 2.3, NCMEC |
| Alt accounts after a block | the same person returning under new names | Persistence and escalation. | 2.4, 3.8 |

### 7.3 "764"/Com-style coercion and self-harm

| Tactic | Example phrasing (paraphrased) | Why it matters | Seen in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Targeting vulnerability | "I know you're sad / nobody cares about you but us" | These groups seek out children with depression, eating disorders or suicidal thoughts. | 3.1, 3.2, 4.1 |
| Group belonging / loyalty tests | "prove you're one of us" / server or group names with numbers or "cult" lore | Initiation through harmful acts. | 3.1, 3.6, 3.9 |
| Ownership language | "you belong to me" / "you're mine now" | Control and humiliation. | 3.2 |
| Self-harm demands | "show me proof" / "write or cut my name on you" | **Urgent.** Seek help and preserve evidence. | 3.1, 4.1, 4.5 |
| Animal cruelty or harming siblings | "hurt your pet / sibling on cam" | Listed in FBI PSAs and the NCA warning. | 3.2, 3.7, 3.10 |
| Doxxing / swatting threats | "I know where you live, I'll send cops" / "I'll threaten your school in your name" | Fear-based compliance; can lead to real police responses. | 3.2, 3.8 |
| Suicide encouragement | "you should just end it" / "do it on stream" / "do it for me" | **Emergency. Escalate immediately (911 / local emergency / crisis line).** | 3.6, 4.2, 4.3 |

### 7.4 Scams and account takeover

| Tactic | Example phrasing (paraphrased) | Why it matters | Seen in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free Nitro / QR | "scan this QR to claim free Nitro" | Approves the scammer's login session. | 5.1 |
| "Accidentally reported" | "I reported you by mistake, DM Discord staff [user] or you'll be banned" | Account takeover; real staff never DM. | 5.2 |
| Fake staff / verification | "I'm Discord Trust & Safety, send me the code we emailed you" | Discord never asks for codes, passwords or tokens. | 5.2 |
| Try my game / cheats | "test my game" (.exe/.zip) / "free Robux script" | Malware that steals accounts and spreads to friends. | 5.3, 5.6 |
| Paste-this-code | "paste this in your browser for free items" | Hands over the session or account. | 5.5 |
| Fake login links | "log in here to see the trade" (lookalike domain) | Phishing ("beaming"). | 5.4 |
| Middleman / off-platform trade | "my friend's a trusted MM, give him your items first" / "I'll PayPal you for your limited" | No recovery from Roblox outside official trades. | 5.7 |
| Password sharing | "give me your login so I can level your account" | Total account loss, and can expose images and DMs. | 5.2, 5.4 |
| Free Robux generator | "get 10k free Robux at [site]" | Always a scam, per Roblox. | 5.7 |

### 7.5 Combination signals (raise severity)
- **Stranger from a game + move to Discord + secrecy + gifts** is the classic grooming sequence (§1).
- **Image request + threat + deadline/payment** is active sextortion. Advise: don't pay, don't delete, report (§2.2).
- **Any self-harm, suicide, animal-harm or "prove it on camera" demand** is a crisis. Escalate immediately (§3–4).
- **A friend's account behaving oddly** (sudden links, files, QR codes, "I reported you") is likely compromised (§5).

## 9. Gaps, caveats and how to use this file

- **Thin areas.**
  - Financial sextortion cases in primary sources that name Discord or Roblox as the extortion channel were rare. Most FBI and NCMEC data points to Instagram and Snapchat, while the FBI notes that schemes often *start* on gaming platforms.
  - No official Indian advisory naming Discord or Roblox was found.
  - Roblox middleman and cross-trading scams appear mainly in parent guides and game-support pages, not government sources.
  - No authoritative source documents Discord "suicide pact" communities. The documented harm is coercion by individuals and 764-type groups.
  - No formal Ofcom penalty against either platform was found.
- **Allegations vs. findings.** State AG suits, the MDL, the wrongful-death suits, the Hindenburg report and indictments (3.5, 3.6, 4.2) are allegations. Only entries marked as pleas or convictions are established.
- **Moving targets.** Platform defaults (Roblox age-banded chat, Discord teen-by-default and age assurance) changed repeatedly in 2024–2026. Don't tell a parent a setting exists without noting that it may have changed.
- **How the assistant should use this.** Cite patterns, not cases, when talking to a parent ("this matches a common grooming step: moving a game contact to private DMs"). Never accuse a named person from one message, and never contact anyone. For threats, image demands, meetup plans or self-harm content, tell the parent to preserve evidence and report (NCMEC CyberTipline / Take It Down, FBI tips.fbi.gov, local police; UK CEOP; Australia eSafety; India 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in), and to seek emergency help where life is at risk.
