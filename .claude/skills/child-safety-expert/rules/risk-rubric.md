# Risk rubric

Use this to rate every contact (handle) and to decide what is urgent. The analyzer's score is only a starting point.

## Levels

| Level | Meaning | Typical evidence | Parent action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | Likely ongoing harm or exploitation; act today | Adult (18+) or unknown-age contact doing any of: sexual talk with/around the child, asking for photos/face/body, secrecy ("don't tell", "keep this between us"), deleting messages, asking where the child lives or for a picture outside the house, offering gifts/Nitro/Robux, wanting to meet. Any sextortion/blackmail. Any request to self-harm or film it. Child's own suicidal plan/goodbye message. | Crisis steps if self-harm; save evidence; report to platform and national channel; don't confront the contact; get professional help |
| **High** | Serious risk that needs a plan this week | Adult in sustained private contact (thousands of messages, late nights) even without sexual content; adults sharing their own sex life with the child; peers pressuring for sexual content; threats, doxxing, sustained harassment; scams involving money, passwords or account takeover; real-money trading; account shared with others or hacked | Talk with the child, adjust settings, leave risky servers, report if criminal |
| **Watch** | Not dangerous by itself, keep an eye | Distressed peers (mutual suicide talk), crude/sexual jokes between same-age teens, substance mentions, heavy late-night use, identifying details shared, romantic online relationship with a same-age teen | Conversation, limits agreed with the child, check again in a few weeks |
| **Low** | Normal | School/real-life friends, short trading chats, bots | None |

**Age gap matters more than words.** The same message ("you're so mature", "send a pic") means something very different from a 14-year-old classmate than from a 19-year-old server owner. Unknown age + adult indicators → treat as adult.

## Escalators (raise one level)
- Contact is an adult or claims to be "older"/in college/working.
- The child is emotionally dependent on the contact (long letters, begging, crisis messages to them).
- Messages are deleted or the contact admits deleting/hiding.
- Late-night share above ~25% in a high-volume chat.
- Contact asked for identifying information and the child gave it.

## De-escalators
- Consistent, verified same-age peer (phone-clock time zone matches stated country, school details consistent over months).
- Contact discourages risky behaviour, tells the child to sleep, suggests adults/helplines.
- Contact is known to the parent in real life.
