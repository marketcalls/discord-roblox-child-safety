# Helplines and reporting channels

Verify numbers before relying on them — services change. If the country isn't listed, use findahelpline.com and the local emergency number.

## Global / platform
- Discord: report in the app (right-click a message → Report); Safety Center discord.com/safety; Family Center (Settings → Family Center).
- Roblox: in-experience Report Abuse; roblox.com/support.
- Take It Down (removing a minor's intimate images, any country): takeitdown.ncmec.org
- Find a crisis line anywhere: findahelpline.com

## India
- Tele-MANAS mental health (24/7, free): **14416** or **1-800-891-4416**
- Emergency: **112** · Childline: **1098**
- Cyber crime: **1930** · cybercrime.gov.in (has a women/children section)

## United States
- Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call/text **988**
- Emergency: **911**
- NCMEC CyberTipline: report.cybertip.org · 1-800-843-5678
- FBI tips: tips.fbi.gov

## United Kingdom
- Samaritans: **116 123** · Childline: **0800 1111** · Shout: text **85258**
- Emergency: **999**
- CEOP (online child sexual abuse): ceop.police.uk · Report Remove (Childline + IWF) for under-18s' images

## Canada
- Suicide crisis line: call/text **988** · Kids Help Phone: **1-800-668-6868** (text 686868)
- Cybertip.ca · NeedHelpNow.ca

## Australia
- Lifeline: **13 11 14** · Kids Helpline: **1800 55 1800**
- Emergency: **000**
- eSafety Commissioner: esafety.gov.au · ACCCE: accce.gov.au/report

## European Union (examples)
- Child helpline: **116 111** (many EU countries) · Emotional support: **116 123** (many countries) · Emergency: **112**
- INHOPE hotlines (report child sexual abuse material): inhope.org

## Pakistan / other South Asia (examples)
- Emergency: Pakistan **15** (police), **1122** (rescue); FIA Cyber Crime: 1991 / complaint.fia.gov.pk
- Use findahelpline.com for crisis lines.
