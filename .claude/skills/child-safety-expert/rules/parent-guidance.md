# Guidance for parents (what to recommend)

## Talking to the child
- Lead with care, not the chats: "I've been worried about you. I love you. How are you really doing?"
- Don't reveal everything you read at once, and never in the middle of a crisis. Plan with a counsellor how and whether to tell them the account was reviewed.
- Don't punish the child for what others did to them, or for confiding in friends.
- Ask open questions about their online world ("explain your server staff job to me") — teens love explaining, and it opens doors.

## "Addiction" and screen time
- Heavy Discord use is usually about **belonging, status and emotional support** (friends at 2 AM, staff roles, romance), not the app itself. Removing it without replacing those needs feels like losing every friend at once.
- **Avoid sudden total bans during distress.** Use staged limits agreed with the child and ideally a clinician:
  1. Sleep first: devices charge outside the bedroom overnight; router/DNS schedule (e.g. NextDNS "Recreation Time" allows an app only at set hours).
  2. Remove the risky parts, keep the safe parts: leave trading/middleman and adult-run servers, resign staff roles properly, DMs to friends only, block high-alert adults.
  3. Replace: real-life friends, sport, clubs, reading, coding, drama — and daily one-to-one time with a parent.
  4. A written family agreement, reviewed weekly.
- DNS blocks only cover home Wi-Fi; mobile data, school Wi-Fi and friends' devices bypass them. Secret use is worse than supervised use.

## Professional help
Recommend a child & adolescent psychiatrist or psychologist whenever there is self-harm, suicidal talk, sudden aggression, or big changes in sleep/school. Share the dated report with them. Teachers and school counsellors can help in confidence. Family therapy if the child reports problems at home.

## Reporting
Adults sexualising a child, sextortion and self-harm coercion are crimes. Save evidence (screenshots with usernames and dates), report on the platform, and to the national channel in `helplines.md`. Don't contact or warn the offender.

## Account hygiene (with the child)
**User Settings → Authorized Apps:** remove unknown apps, especially any that can "Join servers for you" or see the friends list (`verification-bots-and-ban-evasion.md`). Never authorize a bot to get something free. 2FA via authenticator, sign out of all sessions, new password, privacy settings (DMs from friends only, explicit-media filter on, friend requests from friends-of-friends only), Discord Family Center, remove real name/school/city from profiles, no photos showing school, uniform, home or street.
