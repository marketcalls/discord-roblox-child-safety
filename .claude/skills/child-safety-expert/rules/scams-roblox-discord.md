# Scams and money risks (Roblox × Discord)

## Roblox trading, cross-trading and "middleman" servers
- Kids trade in-game items (Royale High, Adopt Me, Blade Ball, Murder Mystery 2 and others) for other games' items, Robux, Discord Nitro or real money. **Cross-trading and real-money trading break Roblox's rules**, so victims of scams have no recourse and accounts get banned.
- Because nobody trusts anyone, trades go through a **middleman (MM)** who holds both sides. Servers run this with ticket bots ("Tickets v2", "Ticket Tool", "Ticket King"). Staff race to "claim" tickets, complete trades and post "proofs". Rank tables tie trust to real money (e.g., "10 tickets → $20 limit … 120 tickets → $250 limit").
- Why it matters: it puts children on staff teams with adults, rewards them with status and gifts, keeps them up through the night (customers in other time zones), and normalises money changing hands with strangers.
- Classic scams: fake middlemen, "trust trades", impersonating a known MM, "I'll pay after", item dupes, chargebacks, account "recovery" services.

## Discord-specific scams
- **Free Nitro / Steam gift** links to look-alike domains (dlscord, discorcl, steamcommunlty, discord-gift-…). Only `discord.gift/…` and the official app are real.
- **QR-code login scam:** "scan this to verify / get a role" — scanning with the Discord app logs the scammer into the account.
- **"I accidentally reported you"** → "contact this Discord staff/Trust & Safety person" → fake staff asks for login, 2FA codes, or payment.
- **Account selling / buying**, "verified" badges, boosts.
- **Token stealers** disguised as games, bots, or "beta testers needed" downloads.
- **Crypto "investment"/doubling**, gift-card resale.

## What to check
- Messages mentioning prices, Robux, Nitro, gift cards, UPI/PayPal/CashApp, "mm", tickets, proofs, limits.
- Whether the child was hacked or shared their password ("my account got hacked", "friends had my login", messages sent from the account the child didn't write).
- Whether adults gift the child things.

## Advice
Turn on 2FA (authenticator app), sign out of all devices, change the password together with the child, set DMs to friends only, leave trading/middleman servers, and agree "no trading for money, no gifts from online strangers".
